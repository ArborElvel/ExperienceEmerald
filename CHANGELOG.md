# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial CurseForge release.

- Added the Infused Emerald item.
- Convert held emeralds in one batch according to the player's available experience.
- Prioritize nearby experience orbs before consuming the player's own experience.
- Allow partial conversion when only part of the held stack is affordable.
- Added safe experience and emerald recovery through the grindstone.
- Added configurable conversion cost, cooldown, orb scan radius, feedback, and grindstone returns.
- Added English and Simplified Chinese localization.
- Licensed the project under LGPL-3.0-or-later.
